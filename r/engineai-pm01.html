PM01

EngineAI · Humanoid · 2025

The EngineAI PM01 (launched Dec 2024) is a ~1.38 m, ~40 kg agile open humanoid with 24 DOF (5/arm, 6/leg), a 320 deg rotating waist and up to 2 m/s walking. Dual-compute Intel N97 + Jetson Orin with RealSense depth and LiDAR; it became known as the first humanoid to perform a front flip, and ships as an open developer platform (ROS/Isaac).
