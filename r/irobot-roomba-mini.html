Roomba Mini

iRobot · Consumer · 2026

Billed as the world's smallest robot vacuum, the Roomba Mini is roughly half the size of a standard Roomba so it can reach corners and gaps larger robots miss. It navigates and maps with iRobot's ClearView LiDAR (working even in the dark), produces up to 70x the suction of the classic 600 series, and pairs with a downsized AutoEmpty Dock. A disposable mop-pad system lets it either vacuum or mop. i
