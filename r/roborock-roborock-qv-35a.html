Qrevo

Roborock · Consumer · 2024

The Roborock Qrevo (QV 35A, 2024) is Roborock's value all-in-one: 8,000 Pa suction with dual spinning mop pads at 200 RPM that apply downward scrubbing pressure, a 350 ml dustbin and 200 ml onboard tank. Its multifunction dock auto-empties (2.5 L bag), washes and dries the mops, and refills from a 3 L clean tank. PreciSense LiDAR, 5,200 mAh, ~180 min.
