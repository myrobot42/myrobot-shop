LaserWeeder

Carbon Robotics · Agricultural · 2023

The Carbon Robotics LaserWeeder uses high-resolution cameras, deep-learning vision and high-power thermal lasers (Class 4) to identify and kill weeds with high crop/weed accuracy - up to ~200,000 weeds/hour - eliminating weeds without chemicals (~80% herbicide reduction). Available as an autonomous unit or tractor implement.
