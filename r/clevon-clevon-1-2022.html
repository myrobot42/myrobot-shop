Clevon 1

Clevon · Delivery · 2022

Clevon 1 (from Estonia's Clevon, formerly Cleveron Mobility) is a compact, road-legal autonomous delivery carrier. Electric and passengerless, it carries modular cargo at up to ~50 km/h under L4 autonomy with teleoperation oversight, targeting grocery and retail last-mile.
