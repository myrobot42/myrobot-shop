tEODor EVO

AeroVironment · Disaster Response · 2019

Latest evolution of the heavy-duty tEODor (telerob Explosive Ordnance Disposal and observation robot), originally built by Telerob of Germany and now part of AeroVironment. A rugged tracked EOD platform with automatic tool-change capability for identifying and disarming IEDs, booby traps and unexploded ordnance in buildings, vehicles and confined areas.
