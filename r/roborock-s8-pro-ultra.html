S8 Pro Ultra

Roborock · Consumer · 2023

Roborock's S8 Pro Ultra (2023) pairs 11,000 Pa suction and dual rubber DuoRoller brushes with a VibraRise 2.0 sonic mop, in a 350 ml-dustbin robot with a 200 ml onboard tank. Its RockDock Ultra auto-empties, washes and dries the mop (60 C), and auto-refills from a 3.5 L clean tank. PreciSense LiDAR navigation, 5,200 mAh battery, ~180 min runtime.
