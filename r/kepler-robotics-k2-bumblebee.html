K2 Bumblebee

Kepler Robotics · Humanoid · 2024

The Kepler K2 'Bumblebee' is the Forerunner K2 industrial humanoid (178 cm, 85 kg, 52 DOF, ~8-hr battery) under its demonstration branding - it debuted at the SAIC-GM automotive plant in Shanghai in April 2025 and made its public appearance at ICRA 2025, aimed at blue-collar industrial automation.
