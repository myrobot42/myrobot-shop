NEXTAGE

Kawada Robotics · Humanoid · 2011

NEXTAGE, from Kawada Robotics (developed out of the HRP program), is a dual-arm production humanoid designed to work alongside people on assembly and production lines. Rather than a walking biped, it's a human-upper-body form on a fixed base: two 6-DOF arms, a 2-DOF head and a 1-DOF torso (15 DOF total), with stereo cameras in its head and a camera in each hand for vision-guided precision assembly
