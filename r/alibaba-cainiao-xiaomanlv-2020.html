Cainiao Xiaomanlv

Alibaba (Cainiao) · Delivery · 2020

Cainiao's Xiaomanlv ('Little Donkey') is Alibaba's L4 autonomous last-mile delivery robot, developed with Alibaba's DAMO Academy. Carrying dozens of parcels per trip (around 100 km of routes per charge), it serves campuses and residential communities across China and has delivered tens of millions of packages.
