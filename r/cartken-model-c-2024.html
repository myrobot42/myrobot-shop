Model C

Cartken · Delivery · 2024

Cartken's six-wheeled Model C is a camera-SLAM autonomous delivery robot (a Google Area 120 spin-off) that operates both outdoors on sidewalks and indoors with elevator/multi-floor capability. It runs L4 autonomy with instant remote-operator override, carries ~20 kg / 30 L, and is assembled by Magna in Michigan.
