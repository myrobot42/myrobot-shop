eBee Geo

AgEagle · Drones · 2016

An affordable fixed-wing mapping drone from AgEagle's senseFly eBee line, aimed at surveyors, civil engineers and GIS professionals. Built on the eBee X airframe (~1.16 m wingspan) but kept light at ~1.3 kg - under the 2 kg line for easier regulatory approval and FAA Remote ID compliant - it hand-launches, belly-lands and maps up to 160 ha (395 ac) in a single 45-minute flight at 120 m.
