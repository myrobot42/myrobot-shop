Rollin' Justin

DLR · Humanoid · 2008

Rollin' Justin, from the German Aerospace Center (DLR), is a landmark mobile-manipulation research humanoid: a torque-controlled humanoid upper body — two 7-DOF DLR-Lightweight-Robot-III arms, two dexterous DLR-Hand-II hands and a 3-DOF movable torso — mounted on an omnidirectional wheeled platform with a variable, extendable base.
