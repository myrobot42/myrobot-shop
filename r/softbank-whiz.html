Whiz

SoftBank Robotics · Service · 2019

The SoftBank Whiz is an autonomous commercial vacuum-sweeping robot (BrainOS-powered) for offices, hotels and retail, learning routes by being driven once then repeating them autonomously; SoftBank's flagship after refocusing from the Pepper humanoid.
