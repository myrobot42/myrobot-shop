Wing (Alphabet) · Drones · 2019

Alphabet's Wing delivery drone is a hybrid VTOL fixed-wing aircraft that hovers to lower packages on a tether without landing. The first drone operator certified by the FAA as an air carrier, Wing has completed 350,000+ deliveries (largely in Australia), carrying ~1.2 kg of food, pharmacy and retail goods on autonomous routes.
