Inspire 3

DJI · Drones · 2023

The DJI Inspire 3 (2023) is a full-frame 8K cinema drone with the Zenmuse X9-8K Air gimbal camera, shooting 8K/25fps CinemaDNG RAW and Apple ProRes RAW. It adds omnidirectional sensing, RTK precision, dual-operator control and O3 Pro transmission to ~15 km, with a transforming airframe for unobstructed cinematic moves.
