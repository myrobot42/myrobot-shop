PUMA 560

Unimation · Industrial · 1978

The PUMA 560 (Programmable Universal Machine for Assembly) is one of the most historically important industrial robots ever built — developed by Victor Scheinman at Unimation in 1978, together with General Motors, and considered the archetypal anthropomorphic robot arm.
