Wingcopter 178 Heavy Lift

Wingcopter · Drones · 2017

The heavy-lift version of Wingcopter's 178, reinforced with glass- and carbon-fibre layers and a more powerful propulsion system to carry heavier professional payloads and sensors while keeping high commonality with the base aircraft. Measuring 48 x 178 x 146 cm and weighing 8.4 kg empty (up to an 18 kg max takeoff weight with four batteries), it lifts up to 6 kg, flies up to ~120 minutes / 120 km
