MUTT 8x8 (S-MET)

General Dynamics Land Systems · Military · 2020

8x8 Multi-Utility Tactical Transport selected as the US Army's Squad Multipurpose Equipment Transport (S-MET) program of record. Carries roughly 1,000 lb (454 kg) of a dismounted squad's load, lightening soldiers' burden, and provides 3 kW (stationary) / 6 kW (moving) of exportable power to recharge radios and batteries. Operates in tethered follow-me, teleoperation and waypoint modes.
