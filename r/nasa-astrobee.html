Astrobee

NASA Ames · Aerospace · 2019

Astrobee is NASA's system of cube-shaped free-flying robots (Bumble, Honey, Queen) aboard the ISS, using electric impeller fans to fly weightlessly and cameras/sensors to navigate the Kibo module - assisting astronauts and hosting student programming challenges.
