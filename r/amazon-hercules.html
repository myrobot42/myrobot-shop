Hercules

Amazon Robotics · AGV/AMR · 2019

Amazon Robotics drive unit — the modern successor to the Kiva drives — that slides under inventory pods and lifts up to ~567 kg (1,250 lb) to bring goods to stationary pickers. A forward-facing 3D camera identifies people, pods and other robots for safe, fast decisions, and thousands coordinate per site as a fleet under centralized planning. First widely deployed mobile robot certified to Safety I
