PARO

AIST · Social · 2004

The original therapeutic social robot. Modeled on a baby harp seal, PARO is the longest-deployed eldercare robot worldwide with documented clinical benefits for dementia patients — reducing agitation and improving mood. FDA-cleared as a Class 2 medical device since 2009.
