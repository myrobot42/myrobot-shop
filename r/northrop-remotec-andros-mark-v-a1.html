ANDROS Mark V-A1

Northrop Grumman · Disaster Response · 2009

Large, highly stable ANDROS variant used as the US military's Remote Ordnance Neutralization System (RONS) for the most demanding hazardous-duty operations. Its variable-speed manipulator lifts up to 100 lb and offers dual-sided quick-change accessory mounts, while a sealed weather-resistant enclosure and articulating tracks let it work in harsh environments and cross ditches and obstacles.
