Laikago

Unitree · Quadruped · 2017

Unitree's first commercial quadruped, launched in 2017 and named after Laika, the first dog in space. A 12-DoF electric platform driven by Unitree's own motors, it established the company's low-cost robot-dog lineage and was the predecessor to the A1 and Aliengo.
