TALOS

PAL Robotics · Humanoid · 2017

PAL Robotics' TALOS (2017) is a 175 cm, 95 kg fully electric, torque-controlled humanoid built for research and industrial manipulation. With 32 DOF and EtherCAT torque-sensing joints, it can lift 6 kg per arm and serves as a high-end ROS research platform.
