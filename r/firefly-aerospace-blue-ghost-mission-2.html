Blue Ghost Mission 2

Firefly Aerospace · Aerospace · 2026

Firefly's second Blue Ghost lunar lander mission, formally named 'Riders 2 the Dark' - America's first mission to the Moon's far side. Uses Firefly's dual-spacecraft configuration with the Blue Ghost lander stacked on the Elytra Dark orbital transfer vehicle, delivering six international payloads including the first radio telescope on the Moon.
