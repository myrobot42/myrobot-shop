Mission Extension Pods (MEPs)

Northrop Grumman · Aerospace · 2025

Northrop Grumman's Mission Extension Pods (MEPs) are small propulsion 'jetpacks' that the Mission Robotic Vehicle attaches to a client satellite to provide years of additional station-keeping - a lighter, lower-cost alternative to a full Mission Extension Vehicle per satellite.
