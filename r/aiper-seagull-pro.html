Seagull Pro

Aiper · Consumer · 2023

Aiper's flagship cordless robotic pool cleaner (model ZT6002) and a CES 2023 Innovation Award honoree. The first cordless pool robot with a quad-motor system — two motors for suction and two for the brushes — plus top-mounted water jets that let it climb and scrub walls and the waterline. WavePath navigation plans systematic coverage of floor, walls and waterline for in-ground pools up to ~1,600 s
