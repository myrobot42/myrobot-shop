Reachy 2

Pollen Robotics · Humanoid · 2024

A fully open-source bimanual humanoid from France's Pollen Robotics (acquired by Hugging Face in April 2025), launched October 2024 as a body for embodied-AI research. Reachy 2 stands 136–166 cm, weighs 50 kg, and pairs two 7-DoF bio-inspired arms — built on Pollen's Orbita 2D/3D compliant joints — each lifting ~3 kg through a parallel torque-controlled gripper.
