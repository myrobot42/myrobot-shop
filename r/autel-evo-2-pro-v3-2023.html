EVO II Pro V3

Autel Robotics · Drones · 2023

The Autel EVO II Pro V3 (2023) is a 6K folding camera drone with a 1-inch 20MP Sony sensor and adjustable f/2.8-f/11 aperture. It offers 40-minute flights, 360-degree obstacle avoidance (19 sensors), SkyLink 2.0 transmission to ~15 km and Level 8 wind resistance - and notably enforces no geofencing, leaving launch authority with the operator.
