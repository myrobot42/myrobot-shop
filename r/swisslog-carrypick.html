CarryPick

Swisslog · AGV/AMR · 2015

Mobile robotic storage-and-picking system: low-profile robots drive beneath mobile racks ('CarryPick shelves'), lift them and carry them to ergonomic pick stations, bringing goods to the operator. Used for automated goods-to-person order fulfillment in e-commerce and healthcare distribution, coordinated by Swisslog's SynQ software.
