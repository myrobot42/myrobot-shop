NEURA Quadruped

NEURA Robotics · Quadruped · 2024

NEURA Robotics' quadruped (Explorer) is a four-legged robot built for challenging terrain, carrying up to ~22 kg with 360-degree vision and running NEURA's Neuraverse OS for autonomous navigation. Targeted at mining, search-and-rescue and rugged industrial inspection, it was recognised with a CES 2026 Innovation Award.
