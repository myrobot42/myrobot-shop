Skilam SCARA

Sankyo Seiki · Industrial · 1981

The Sankyo Seiki Skilam is one of the first commercial SCARA robots — the SCARA concept (Selective Compliance Assembly Robot Arm) was invented by Professor Hiroshi Makino at the University of Yamanashi around 1978-1981, and Sankyo Seiki was among the pioneering manufacturers (with the Makino lab) to commercialise it as the Skilam line.
