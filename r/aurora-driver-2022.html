Aurora Driver

Aurora Innovation · Autonomous Vehicles · 2022

The Aurora Driver is a Level 4 autonomous trucking platform that launched driverless commercial freight between Dallas and Houston in April 2024. Its multi-modal suite of 25+ sensors gives a 360 degree view, anchored by proprietary FirstLight FMCW long-range lidar that detects objects beyond 450 m (next-gen kit extends to 1 km), plus custom imaging radar and high-resolution cameras.
