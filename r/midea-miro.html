MIRO

Midea · Humanoid · 2025

MIRO is Midea Group's industrial humanoid robot, part of the Chinese appliance giant's humanoid matrix (built on its 2017 KUKA acquisition and its Humanoid Robot Innovation Centre). It targets manipulation, navigation and automated industrial tasks.
