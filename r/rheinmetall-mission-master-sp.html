Mission Master SP

Rheinmetall · Military · 2018

Low-profile 8x8 all-electric autonomous UGV (Silent Partner) built for forward and last-mile resupply, silent watch and carriage of section sensors or weapon systems. Weighs 1,134 kg, carries up to 1,000 kg (300 kg amphibious) and reaches 40 km/h, using Rheinmetall's PATH autonomy kit for follow-me, teleoperation and supervised autonomy. Networked with Rheinmetall's soldier system and C2 software;
