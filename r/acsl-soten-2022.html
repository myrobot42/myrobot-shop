SOTEN

ACSL · Drones · 2022

ACSL's SOTEN (Sky-Hawk) is a Japanese-made small drone developed as a security-conscious alternative to foreign drones for government and infrastructure use. With swappable 4K/thermal/multispectral payloads, encrypted data handling and domestic supply-chain assurance, it serves inspection, security and disaster-response roles.
