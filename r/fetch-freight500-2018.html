Freight500

Fetch Robotics · AGV/AMR · 2018

Heavy autonomous mobile robot base for cases and smaller palletized loads — carries up to 500 kg on a 1265×838 mm surface (fits EUR6 pallets) through 40-inch-wide aisles. Maps and plans its own routes with dynamic obstacle avoidance and a certified hardware safety system, configured via the FetchCore cloud platform. Now part of Zebra Technologies.
