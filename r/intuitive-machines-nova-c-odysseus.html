Nova-C (Odysseus)

Intuitive Machines · Aerospace · 2024

The Intuitive Machines Nova-C "Odysseus" was the first US lunar lander since Apollo and the first commercial soft landing on the Moon (IM-1, Feb 2024). A ~1,900 kg hexagonal-cylinder lander (100 kg payload) that touched down near the lunar south pole, tipping on landing but returning data.
