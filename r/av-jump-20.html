JUMP 20

AeroVironment · Drones · 2018

A runway-independent VTOL fixed-wing medium (Group 3) UAS for persistent multi-sensor ISR, derived from the Arcturus T-20 (AeroVironment acquired Arcturus in 2021). Four boom-mounted electric rotors handle vertical takeoff and landing while a heavy-fuel/MOGAS EFI engine drives efficient forward cruise, so it needs no runway or launcher and sets up in under 30-60 minutes with a two-person crew.
