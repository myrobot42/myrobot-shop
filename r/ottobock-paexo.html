Paexo Shoulder

Ottobock · Exoskeletons · 2019

The Ottobock Paexo Shoulder is an ultralight (~1.9 kg) passive exoskeleton that mechanically transfers overhead-work arm loads to the hips, reducing shoulder strain in assembly and construction. Part of the Paexo family (Back, Thumb, Neck).
