Litter-Robot 4

Whisker · Consumer · 2021

The Whisker Litter-Robot 4 is a self-cleaning automatic litter box that sifts and rotates to separate waste after each use, with OmniSense weight/litter-level sensing that tracks each cat's weight and litter level, reporting to the Whisker app over Wi-Fi.
