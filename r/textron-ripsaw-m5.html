Ripsaw M5

Textron Systems · Military · 2019

Fifth-generation Ripsaw — a tracked Robotic Combat Vehicle developed by Team Ripsaw (Textron Systems, Howe & Howe and Teledyne FLIR) for the US Army's RCV-Medium effort. Hybrid diesel-electric drivetrain, flat open architecture and configurable armour, with a 10.5-tonne combat weight, ~3,600 kg payload and 65 km/h top speed. Can mount a Kongsberg MCT-30 turret (30/40 mm Bushmaster), carry the R80D
