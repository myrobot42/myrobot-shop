Hugo RAS System

Medtronic · Medical · 2021

The Medtronic Hugo RAS is a modular robotic-assisted surgery system with independent arm carts and an open console. It received US FDA clearance for urologic procedures in December 2025 (CE-marked since 2021, used in 30+ countries), with general and gynecologic submissions to follow - the first major soft-tissue robotic rival to da Vinci in the US in two decades.
