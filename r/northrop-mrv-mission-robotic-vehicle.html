Mission Robotic Vehicle (MRV)

Northrop Grumman · Aerospace · 2025

Northrop Grumman's Mission Robotic Vehicle (MRV) is a next-generation servicing spacecraft equipped with robotic arms (developed with DARPA) to inspect client satellites and install Mission Extension Pods, enabling a scalable in-orbit life-extension service rather than one dedicated servicer per client.
