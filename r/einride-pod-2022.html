Einride Pod

Einride · Autonomous Vehicles · 2022

The Einride Pod is a cabless, all-electric autonomous freight vehicle - it has no driver compartment at all. Operating at Level 4 with a single remote operator able to supervise multiple Pods, it carries up to ~16 tonnes of cargo on battery-electric power (~180 km range) using NVIDIA DRIVE compute and a lidar-based sensor suite.
