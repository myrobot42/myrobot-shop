Baxter

Rethink Robotics · Industrial · 2012

Rethink Robotics' Baxter (2012) was a landmark in collaborative robotics — the robot that popularised the idea of a fenceless, teach-by-demonstration arm that ordinary factory workers could program by grabbing and moving it.
