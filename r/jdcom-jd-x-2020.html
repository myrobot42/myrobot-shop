JD X

JD.com · Delivery · 2020

JD.com's autonomous delivery robot (from its X division / JD Logistics) is an electric, passengerless L4 vehicle for last-mile e-commerce parcel delivery. Lidar-and-camera guided, it carries multiple lockered parcels to neighbourhoods and was among the first deployed at scale in China, including during the COVID period in Wuhan.
