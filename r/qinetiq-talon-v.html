TALON V

QinetiQ · Disaster Response · 2023

QinetiQ's TALON V is the modernised sixth-generation of the world-renowned TALON family, with 4,000+ units delivered. An IOP-first, modular tracked EOD/CBRN robot with heavy-lift manipulator, 16 I/O ports, HD and thermal cameras and JAUS interoperability, it performs bomb disposal, CBRN/HazMat reconnaissance, surveillance and comms-relay missions.
