FLIP Rover

Astrolab · Aerospace · 2026

Venturi Astrolab's FLEX Lunar Innovation Platform (FLIP) - a roughly half-tonne precursor rover carrying 30kg of payload to the lunar south pole aboard Astrobotic's Griffin-1 lander. A stripped-down flight testbed for Astrolab's larger FLEX vehicle, carrying flight-ready Venturi batteries, hyper-deformable wheels and the same avionics and autonomous navigation software as FLEX.
