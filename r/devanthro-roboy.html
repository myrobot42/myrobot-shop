Roboy

Devanthro · Humanoid · 2013

Roboy, from Devanthro (originating at TU Munich / Fortiss in 2013), is an open-source tendon-driven musculoskeletal humanoid built to mimic the human muscle-tendon system rather than use conventional rigid joints.
