ADRAS-J

Astroscale · Aerospace · 2024

Astroscale's ADRAS-J (Active Debris Removal by Astroscale-Japan, 2024) rendezvoused with and closely inspected a derelict Japanese H-IIA rocket upper stage - the first commercial mission to safely approach and survey a large piece of real space debris, a key step toward future removal.
