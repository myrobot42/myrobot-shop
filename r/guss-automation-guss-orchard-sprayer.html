GUSS Autonomous Orchard Sprayer

GUSS Automation · Agricultural · 2018

The original GUSS (Global Unmanned Spray System) - a four-wheel-drive diesel autonomous orchard sprayer navigating tree rows by GPS, LiDAR and cameras. One operator supervises up to eight machines from a laptop. Created by orchard-spraying business owner Dave Crinklaw in response to agricultural labour shortage; now owned by John Deere.
