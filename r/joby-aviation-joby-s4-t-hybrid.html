Joby S4-T Hybrid

Joby Aviation · Military · 2025

The Joby S4-T is a tactical/hybrid variant of Joby Aviation's S4 eVTOL - a six-tilt-rotor aircraft carrying four passengers plus pilot at up to ~322 km/h. All-electric with a hydrogen-electric hybrid demonstrator extending range well beyond the baseline ~240 km, it is being evaluated by the US DoD under the Air Force's Agility Prime programme.
