Wayve Driver

Wayve · Autonomous Vehicles · 2024

Wayve Driver is an embodied-AI, end-to-end driving system (an 'AV2.0' approach) that learns to drive from data rather than relying on HD maps or hand-coded rules. It is camera-first and hardware-agnostic, can run across different vehicle platforms, and is powered by Wayve's GAIA world model.
