AIBO ERS-110

Sony · Quadruped · 1999

The first AIBO — Sony’s entertainment robot dog sold out its initial 3,000 Japanese units in 20 minutes in May 1999. With 18 joints, a camera nose and learned behaviors, ERS-110 created the consumer robot-companion category and remains a design icon.
