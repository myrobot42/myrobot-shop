Kepler Forerunner K1

Kepler Robotics · Humanoid · 2023

Kepler's Forerunner K1 (Shanghai Kepler, Nov 2023) was the company's first full-size humanoid, with in-house roller-screw arm/leg actuators, custom rotary waist/shoulder joints, an 8-hour battery and sensor-packed hands handling up to ~25 kg, taking aim at Tesla's Optimus at CES 2024.
