TUG T4

Aethon · AGV/AMR · 2019

Industrial open-platform variant of the TUG from ST Engineering Aethon, built for manufacturing and warehouse material flow. An independent suspension system handles uneven floors, with roughly 5° slope capability and a tight ~17-inch turning radius. The open deck integrates with active payloads and conveyors.
