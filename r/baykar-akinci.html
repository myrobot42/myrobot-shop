Akıncı

Baykar · Drones · 2021

Baykar's flagship high-altitude, long-endurance unmanned combat aircraft (Bayraktar Akinci), a much larger sibling to the TB2. It's a twin-turboprop design - typically 450 hp AI-450T engines (with PD-170 and TP-395 variants) - spanning about 20 m with a ~12.2 m length and ~5,500 kg max takeoff weight, able to carry roughly 1,350 kg of payload across six hardpoints (internal and external).
