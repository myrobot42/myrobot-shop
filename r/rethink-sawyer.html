Sawyer

Rethink Robotics · Industrial · 2015

Rethink Robotics' single-arm successor to Baxter: a 7-DoF high-precision collaborative arm with series-elastic actuators, embedded Cognex vision and an animated LCD face. Built for machine tending and precision tasks. After Rethink folded in 2018, the Sawyer design and IP were acquired and the robot continued under HAHN Group.
