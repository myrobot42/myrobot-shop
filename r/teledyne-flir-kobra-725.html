Kobra 725

Teledyne FLIR · Military · 2019

The Teledyne FLIR Kobra 725 is a heavy-payload UGV with a high-strength manipulator arm for lifting heavy loads, an HD PTZ camera mast and stair-climbing mobility. IOP-compliant and highly modular, it integrates EOD, CBRN, breaching, mine-clearance, X-ray and LIDAR accessories for the most demanding disposal tasks.
