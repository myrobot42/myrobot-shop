Ottava

Johnson & Johnson MedTech · Medical · 2024

Ottava is Johnson & Johnson MedTech's soft-tissue surgical robot, uniquely integrating four arms into the operating table for a low profile and flexible setup. First US clinical cases (incl. gastric bypass) were completed in 2025, with an FDA submission expected in early 2026.
