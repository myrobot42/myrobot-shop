Mavic 4 Pro

DJI · Drones · 2025

DJI's Mavic 4 Pro (2025) is the flagship consumer camera drone: a triple-camera Hasselblad system led by a 100MP 4/3 sensor plus 48MP and 50MP telephoto lenses, shooting 6K HDR video on a 360-degree-rotating Infinity Gimbal. It flies up to 51 minutes, transmits via O4+ to ~30 km, weighs 1.06 kg and adds omnidirectional sensing and intelligent tracking.
