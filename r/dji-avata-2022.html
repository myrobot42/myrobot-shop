Avata

DJI · Drones · 2022

The DJI Avata (2022) is a compact cinewhoop FPV drone with built-in propeller guards for safe close-proximity and indoor flying. It shoots stabilised 4K/60fps video with RockSteady, flies via goggles with motion control, and recovers to a hover with its emergency brake - FPV cinematography for beginners.
