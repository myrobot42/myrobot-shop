WALK-MAN

IIT · Humanoid · 2017

WALK-MAN, from the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT), is a rugged full-size disaster-response research humanoid developed under an EU project launched in the wake of the Fukushima nuclear accident to explore humanoids that could intervene in hazardous, human-engineered environments.
