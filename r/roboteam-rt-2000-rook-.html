RT-2000 (ROOK)

RoboTeam · Military · 2019

RoboTeam's ROOK (RT-2000) is a modular tracked UGV that hosts mission payloads for logistics, ISR and EOD/route-clearance tasks. Part of RoboTeam's interoperable family controlled from the ROCU, it brings heavier payload capacity than the backpackable MTGR while keeping rapid deployability.
