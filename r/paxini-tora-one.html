TORA-ONE

PaXini · Humanoid · 2026

TORA-ONE is a wheeled humanoid robot from Shenzhen-based PaXini Tech, a tactile-sensing specialist backed by a 100-million-yuan strategic investment from BYD. Built around PaXini's 6D Hall-array tactile technology, it carries roughly 1,956 tactile sensors (7,824 channels) alongside five RGB monocular cameras, two depth cameras, fisheye vision and LiDAR with laser SLAM.
