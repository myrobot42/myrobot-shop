Prosthesis

Furrion Exo-Bionics · Mecha · 2017

A 3.6-tonne electric racing animal. Prosthesis is pure pilot skill: your limbs drive its four giant legs through direct mechanical amplification — no AI, no balance software. Built by Furrion Exo-Bionics in Canada as the founding machine of a future mech-racing league, it holds the Guinness record for largest tetrapod exoskeleton and genuinely runs.
