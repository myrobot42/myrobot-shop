Stuntronics

Walt Disney Imagineering · Entertainment · 2018

Stuntronics is Disney Imagineering's autonomous acrobatic robot - a self-correcting aerial performer that can flip and strike heroic poses mid-air, used for stunt doubles of characters (e.g. Spider-Man over Avengers Campus). It senses its body angle and rotation in flight to land poses precisely.
