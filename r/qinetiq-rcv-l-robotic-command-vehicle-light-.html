RCV-L (Robotic Command Vehicle - Light)

QinetiQ · Military · 2020

QinetiQ's RCV-L (Robotic Combat Vehicle-Light), developed with Pratt Miller, is a ~3.9-tonne diesel-electric hybrid, rubber-tracked unmanned combat vehicle for the US Army's RCV programme. With a modular payload architecture carrying ~3,175 kg at over 60 km/h, it can be remotely controlled or driven semi-autonomously and is air-transportable to remote locations.
