V9e

iLife · Consumer · 2020

An affordable, vacuum-only robot from iLife's pet-focused V-series, built around raw suction rather than smart mapping. Delivers up to 4,000 Pa through a cyclone system into a large 750 mL dustbin, with brushless (roller-free) design to reduce hair tangles and 3-stage filtration capturing 99.95% of 0.3 µm particles. Navigates in a randomised zig-zag pattern (no LiDAR) and returns to dock to rechar
