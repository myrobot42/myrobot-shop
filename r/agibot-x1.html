X1 (Lingxi X1)

AGIBOT · Humanoid · 2024

The AGIBOT X1 (Lingxi X1) is a fully open-source modular humanoid robot: 130 cm, 33 kg, 34 degrees of freedom on PowerFlow quasi-direct-drive servos (R86-3 actuator up to 200 N.m peak), walking at ~1 m/s with a 2-hour runtime. Built on the AimRT framework with reinforcement-learning locomotion and free design/software access, it is a benchtop-friendly platform for research and maker communities.
