Stanley

Stanford Racing Team · Autonomous Vehicles · 2005

The self-driving VW Touareg from Stanford (Sebastian Thrun's team) that won the 2005 DARPA Grand Challenge, completing a 212 km desert course autonomously. Stanley's LIDAR + machine-learning terrain approach is widely credited with kick-starting the modern self-driving-car industry.
