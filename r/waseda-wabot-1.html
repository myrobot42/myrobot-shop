WABOT-1

Waseda University · Humanoid · 1973

WABOT-1, completed in 1973 at Waseda University under Professor Ichiro Kato ('father of Japanese robotics'), is recognised as the world's first full-scale anthropomorphic (humanoid) robot — the machine that began the entire quest to build robots in human form.
