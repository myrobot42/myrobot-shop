WABOT-1

Waseda University · Humanoid · 1973

Completed in 1973 at Tokyo's Waseda University under Professor Ichiro Kato (the 'father of Japanese robotics'), WABOT-1 is widely recognised as the world's first full-scale anthropomorphic robot.
