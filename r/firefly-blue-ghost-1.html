Blue Ghost Mission 1

Firefly Aerospace · Aerospace · 2025

First fully successful commercial lunar landing in history. Landed upright in Mare Crisium near Mons Latreille on 2 March 2025, completing all primary mission objectives over 14 Earth days. Operated 10 NASA CLPS science payloads on the lunar surface. First mission to capture a GPS lock from the Moon and high-resolution video of a lunar sunset. Powered down for lunar night on 16 March 2025.
