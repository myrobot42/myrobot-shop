Walt Disney – A Magical Life

Walt Disney Imagineering · Entertainment · 2025

Audio-Animatronic figure of Walt Disney himself, opened July 17 2025 at Disneyland California for the park's 70th anniversary. Plays in rotation with Great Moments with Mr. Lincoln (1964). 17-minute stage show in the Main Street, USA section. Controversial — Walt's granddaughter Joanna Miller publicly opposed the robot likeness, calling it 'dehumanizing.'
