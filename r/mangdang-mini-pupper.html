Mini Pupper

MangDang · Quadruped · 2021

An affordable open-source quadruped (from ~$499) built on Raspberry Pi and ROS, derived from Stanford's Pupper project. Mini Pupper walks, does SLAM mapping and runs AI demos, making legged-robot and ROS education accessible to students and makers.
