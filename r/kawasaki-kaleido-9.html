Kaleido 9 (RHP)

Kawasaki · Humanoid · 2025

The Kawasaki Kaleido 9, unveiled at iREX 2025 in Tokyo, is the ninth generation of Kawasaki's RHP Kaleido humanoid line (in development since 2015). At 1,900 mm and 99 kg, it's taller and heavier than earlier Kaleidos but far more capable: engineers significantly reinforced the waist and leg joints to solve the classic bipedal problem of balancing under load, enabling it to lift 30 kg.
