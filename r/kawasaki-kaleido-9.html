Kaleido 9

Kawasaki · Humanoid · 2025

The 9th generation of Kawasaki's RHP (Robust Humanoid Platform) Kaleido, unveiled at iREX 2025 in Tokyo. Building on a program running since 2015, Kaleido 9 emphasises robustness and real-world utility over acrobatics: reinforced waist and leg joints improve balance under load, and payload rose ~35% to 18 kg.
