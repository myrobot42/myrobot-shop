CADRE Rovers

NASA JPL · Aerospace · 2026

CADRE (Cooperative Autonomous Distributed Robotic Explorers) is a set of small shoebox-sized NASA rovers designed to operate together autonomously as a team on the Moon, demonstrating multi-robot coordination; manifested on the IM-3 mission.
