Matrice 350 RTK

DJI · Drones · 2023

The DJI Matrice 350 RTK (2023) is a flagship enterprise drone platform: IP55-rated, 55-minute flight time, six-directional obstacle sensing and RTK precision, with O3 Enterprise transmission to ~20 km. It carries up to ~2.7 kg of interchangeable payloads (Zenmuse H30, L2, P1) for inspection, mapping, public safety and SAR.
