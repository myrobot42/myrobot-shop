eBee TAC

AgEagle · Drones · 2019

A cyber-secure, tactical version of AgEagle's (senseFly) eBee X fixed-wing mapping drone, aimed at public-safety and defense users. Carried in a backpack, hand-launched and deployed in 3 minutes by one person, it delivers 2D, 3D and thermal aerial data over large areas - up to 500 ha (1,235 ac) in a single 90-minute flight with a 55 km range - at 1.5 cm accuracy using optional RTK/PPK.
