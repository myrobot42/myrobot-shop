MIFIK Autonomy Kit

Milrem Robotics · Military · 2019

MIFIK is Milrem's Autonomy Functionalities Kit - a modular hardware/software package that adds autonomous capabilities (follow-me, waypoint navigation, obstacle detection) to Milrem platforms and third-party vehicles, forming the unified autonomy layer across THeMIS, HAVOC and Type-X.
