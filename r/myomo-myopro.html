MyoPro

Myomo · Exoskeletons · 2018

The Myomo MyoPro is a powered myoelectric upper-limb orthosis that senses faint EMG signals from a weakened or paralysed arm and assists elbow and hand movement, helping people with stroke, brachial plexus injury or ALS regain arm function.
