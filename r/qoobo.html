Qoobo

Yukai Engineering · Social · 2018

Therapeutic robotic cushion with a moving tail. No face, no voice — just a tail that responds to touch like a cat. Designed for people who can't keep pets (apartments, allergies, eldercare). Won multiple design awards including Red Dot.
