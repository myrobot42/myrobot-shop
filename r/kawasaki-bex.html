Bex

Kawasaki · Quadruped · 2022

A rideable, goat-inspired quadruped unveiled at the 2022 International Robot Exhibition in Tokyo, part of Kawasaki's Robust Humanoid Platform (RHP) initiative — the same program behind the Kaleido humanoid. Bex walks on four legs or rolls on retractable knee wheels, has a modular upper body, and is aimed at cargo carrying on farms and construction sites.
