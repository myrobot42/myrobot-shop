Z-Machines

Z-Machines · Entertainment · 2013

Japanese all-robot band consisting of MACH (78-finger guitarist), ASHURA (22-arm drummer), and COSMO (laser keyboardist). Created by Naofumi Yonetsuka (Tokyo University), performed at SXSW 2013 and Tokyo. Squarepusher composed 'Music for Robots' EP specifically for them in 2014. Project paused after 2015.
