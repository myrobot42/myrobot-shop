Serve Gen 1

Serve Robotics · Delivery · 2021

Serve Robotics' first-generation low-emission sidewalk delivery robot, spun out of Postmates/Uber. A four-wheeled L4 bot with fail-safe mechanical braking and autonomous collision avoidance, it delivers food on sidewalks with remote human assist, deployed initially with Uber Eats in Los Angeles.
