RB-Y1

Rainbow Robotics · Humanoid · 2024

The Rainbow Robotics RB-Y1 is Korea's first bimanual mobile manipulator — a humanoid-type dual-arm system (two 7-DOF arms) mounted, via a 6-DOF single leg that raises the body more than 50 cm, on a high-speed wheeled base, for 24 total DOF under 20-axis whole-body control.
