Cog

MIT AI Lab · Humanoid · 1994

Cog was a landmark embodied-AI research humanoid built by Rodney Brooks's group at the MIT AI Lab from 1994 — an upper-body robot (torso, head, two arms; ~22 DOF on a fixed base) created to test a radical hypothesis: that human-like intelligence can only emerge from a human-like body interacting with the world, not from disembodied computation.
