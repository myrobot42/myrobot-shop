Xingzhe Taishan (行者泰山)

Yobotics · Humanoid · 2025

Xingzhe Taishan (行者泰山) is a 1.38 m humanoid robot developed by Yobotics and fielded by Shandong's Taishan Team. It first drew attention at the 2025 World Humanoid Robot Games, where it kept running even after losing an arm.
