Xingzhe Taishan (行者泰山)

Yobotics · Humanoid · 2025

Xingzhe Taishan took first place in the 100-meter exhibition race at the 2025 World Humanoid Robot Games, and demonstrated strong balance and disturbance recovery — staying upright even when pushed or kicked.
