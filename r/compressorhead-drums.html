Stickboy (Compressorhead drummer)

Compressorhead · Entertainment · 2007

Four-armed pneumatic drummer in the world's first robotic heavy metal band Compressorhead. Built by German engineers from steel auto parts. Has played live across Europe and Australia, opened for Black Sabbath at Wacken Open Air 2016. The full band includes Bones (bass), Fingers (guitar), and Hellga (vocals).
