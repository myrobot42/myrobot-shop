iCub

IIT (Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia) · Research · 2018

iCub is an open-source child-sized (~104 cm) humanoid research platform from the Italian Institute of Technology, with ~53 DOF, rich tactile skin and vision, used worldwide to study embodied cognition, manipulation and human-robot interaction.
