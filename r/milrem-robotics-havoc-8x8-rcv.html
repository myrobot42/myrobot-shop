HAVOC 8x8 RCV

Milrem Robotics · Military · 2023

Milrem's HAVOC is an 8x8 wheeled robotic combat vehicle (unveiled 2025) weighing ~15 tonnes with a 5-tonne payload. Its hybrid-electric driveline (near-silent, instant torque) delivers 110 km/h on road and 600 km range, with AI-driven navigation and electronic-warfare resilience, designed to support armoured formations and mount weapons up to 30 mm.
