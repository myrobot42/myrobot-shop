Mission Master XT

Rheinmetall · Military · 2021

Rugged 4x4 high-mobility A-UGV based on the Zeal Motor Fat Truck platform, carrying up to 1,000 kg without compromising amphibious capability and offering a 750 km range. Integrated Li-ion batteries give around six hours of silent-watch endurance, and the design follows NATO STANAGs and US MIL-STD-6017 for battle-management interoperability. Can be parachute-delivered and underslung by CH-53/CH-47
