Vector

Quantum Systems · Drones · 2023

Quantum Systems' Vector is a dual-use fixed-wing VTOL drone for ISR, mapping and security, with ~2 hours endurance and modular EO/IR payloads. Vertical takeoff and landing plus long winged endurance make it suited to both commercial survey and defense reconnaissance, including use in Ukraine.
