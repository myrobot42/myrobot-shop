Viking 1 Lander

NASA · Aerospace · 1976

First spacecraft to successfully soft-land on Mars and complete its mission. Equipped with a 3-meter robotic surface sampler arm that scooped Martian soil into onboard biology, chemistry, and meteorology instruments. Operated for over 6 years on the surface, returning more than 4,500 images and groundbreaking data that defined Mars science for decades.
