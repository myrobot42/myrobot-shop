GoMate

GAC Group · Humanoid · 2024

The third-generation embodied-intelligence humanoid from Chinese automaker GAC (Guangzhou Automobile Group), unveiled December 2024. Its signature is an industry-first variable wheel-leg base that switches between a stable four-wheel mode (1.4 m tall, for stairs, slopes and obstacles) and an agile two-wheel mode (1.75 m tall, smaller footprint) — a design GAC says cuts energy use by over 80%.
