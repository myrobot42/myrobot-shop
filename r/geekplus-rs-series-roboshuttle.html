RS-Series RoboShuttle

Geekplus · AGV/AMR · 2019

Rack-climbing tote-to-person shuttle that retrieves totes from vertical storage up to 5 m, 8 m or 11 m and delivers them to pick stations, improving picking efficiency 2–3×. Supports up to ~350 totes/hour/station and connects to conveyors and other Geek+ robots; part of the RoboShuttle solution scaling to 12 m of storage.
