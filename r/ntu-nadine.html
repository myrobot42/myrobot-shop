Nadine

Nanyang Technological University · Humanoid · 2015

Nadine, unveiled in 2015 at Singapore's Nanyang Technological University, is a lifelike social android modelled on Professor Nadia Magnenat Thalmann — a pioneer of 'virtual humans' who directed NTU's Institute for Media Innovation and founded the University of Geneva's MIRALab. Its realistic hardware (silicone skin, articulated hands for natural grasping) was built by Japanese animatronics firm Ko
