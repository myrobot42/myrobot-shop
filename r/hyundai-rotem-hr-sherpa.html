HR-Sherpa

Hyundai Rotem · Military · 2020

Multipurpose wheeled (6x6) unmanned ground vehicle from Hyundai Rotem of South Korea, designed for surveillance and reconnaissance, supply transport, casualty evacuation and fire support. Electrically driven with autonomous follow and remote-control modes, it can mount a remote weapon station and is part of Korea's push toward manned-unmanned teaming for its ground forces.
