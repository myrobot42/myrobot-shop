Mission Master CXT

Rheinmetall · Military · 2021

Rugged 4x4 diesel-electric hybrid UGV built for extreme terrain, carrying its full 1,000 kg payload with advanced amphibious capability. Combines a diesel engine with a silent electric motor for a 450 km total range (including 50 km battery-only), and is internally transportable by CH-47, CH-53 and C-130 aircraft. Shares the Mission Master autonomy and C2 suite with the rest of the family.
