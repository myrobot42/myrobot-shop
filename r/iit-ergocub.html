ergoCub

IIT · Humanoid · 2023

ergoCub, from the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT), is an ergonomically-designed collaborative humanoid — an evolution of IIT's famous iCub — built specifically to reduce musculoskeletal injuries among workers.
