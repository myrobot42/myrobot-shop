Elf-Xuan 2.0

AheadForm · Social · 2025

Elf-Xuan 2.0 is a hyper-realistic expressive humanoid from Shanghai startup AheadForm, built around a static sculpted body and a richly animated head. Ultra-quiet brushless micro-motors drive roughly 30 facial 'muscles' beneath a modular, magnetically-attached silicone skin (a later gala build reportedly used 42 independent facial motors), producing lifelike micro-expressions and gaze.
