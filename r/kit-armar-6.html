ARMAR-6

KIT · Humanoid · 2018

ARMAR-6, from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology's High Performance Humanoid Technologies (H2T) lab led by Tamim Asfour, is a high-performance collaborative research humanoid built for human-robot collaboration in real-world scenarios — famously demonstrated proactively assisting maintenance workers, recognising when a coworker needs a tool and handing it over.
