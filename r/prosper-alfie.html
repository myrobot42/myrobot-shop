Alfie

Prosper Robotics · Humanoid · 2025

A general-purpose home 'butler' robot from London's Prosper Robotics, founded in 2021 by ex-OpenAI engineer Shariq Hashme (with prototyping in Shenzhen). Alfie stands about 1.7 m and weighs over 100 kg, riding a wheeled base with two arms and pincher hands — legs were dropped as unnecessarily complex, with lifts used for stairs.
