Tiangong 3.0 (天工 3.0)

Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center · Humanoid · 2026

Tiangong 3.0 (天工 3.0, 'Tien Kung 3.0') is the next-generation general-purpose humanoid platform from the Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center, unveiled in February 2026 as a successor to the marathon-winning Tiangong line. Like its predecessors it is offered as an open platform, and teams of 'secondary developers' fielded customised Tiangong 3.0 robots at the 2026 Beijing Humanoid Half-Maratho
