Elios 3

Flyability · Drones · 2022

Flyability's Elios 3 is a collision-tolerant drone wrapped in a protective cage for inspecting confined and hazardous industrial spaces - tanks, boilers, tunnels and silos. Its FlyAware LiDAR-SLAM system builds real-time 3D maps in GPS-denied interiors, with a 4K camera and optional thermal payload for safe remote inspection.
