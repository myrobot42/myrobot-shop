Switchblade 300

AeroVironment · Drones · 2018

AeroVironment's Switchblade 300 is a man-portable, tube-launched loitering munition - a backpackable 'kamikaze' drone with ~15 minutes endurance and ~10 km range that streams video before diving onto a target with a small anti-personnel warhead and a wave-off/abort capability. Widely fielded by the US military and supplied to Ukraine.
