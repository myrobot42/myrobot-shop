Unimate

Unimation · Industrial · 1961

The Unimate is the world's first industrial robot — conceived by George Devol (who patented 'Programmed Article Transfer' in 1954) and commercialised by Joseph Engelberger through Unimation, with the first unit installed on a General Motors die-casting line in Ewing Township, New Jersey, in 1961.
