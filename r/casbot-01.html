CASBOT 01

CASBOT · Humanoid · 2024

Nicknamed "Wednesday", CASBOT 01 is a full-size bipedal humanoid (1.79 m, 60 kg, 52 DoF) unveiled Nov 2024 and shown at CES 2025. 550 TOPS of compute, 4+ hour runtime, five-fingered dexterous hands and high-dynamic force control. Developed by Beijing's Lingbao CASBOT with Lenovo's Fire Seed Project; first deployment is precise micro-assembly at a Lenovo laptop factory.
