Astronaut A5

Lely · Agricultural · 2018

The Lely Astronaut A5 is an automatic (robotic) milking system that lets cows be milked voluntarily on demand; a robotic arm with 3D laser-guided teat detection attaches the cups while the system tracks per-cow yield and health data.
