AELOS LITE

Leju Robotics · Humanoid · 2018

The Leju AELOS Lite is a 17-DOF programmable humanoid built for classroom STEM learning - it walks, dances, boxes and plays soccer, with a head that rotates ~160 degrees. Block-based Scratch/Blockly programming makes it approachable for beginners, and its torque-composite servos give long life and precise motion.
