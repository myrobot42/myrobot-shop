HUBO-2 (KHR-4)

Rainbow Robotics · Humanoid · 2009

HUBO-2 (KHR-4, also 'Jaemi HUBO'), developed 2008-2009 by Professor Jun-Ho Oh's HUBO Lab at KAIST in South Korea, is a landmark full-size humanoid — one of the first advanced full-body humanoids developed outside Japan.
