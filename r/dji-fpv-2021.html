The DJI FPV (2021) brought immersive first-person-view flying to the mainstream, pairing low-latency goggles with a 4K/60fps single-axis-stabilised camera. It hits ~140 km/h in Manual mode yet keeps GPS, obstacle sensing and an emergency brake-and-hover for safety, making FPV accessible to newcomers.
