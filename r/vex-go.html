VEX GO

VEX Robotics · Educational · 2021

An affordable snap-together STEM construction system from VEX Robotics aimed at ages 7–11, designed so young students can tinker, make mistakes and try again. The education kit packs 296 structural and motion components with a GO Robot Brain, three motors, three electromagnets, an eye (optical) sensor, an LED bumper switch, a rechargeable battery and storage cases.
