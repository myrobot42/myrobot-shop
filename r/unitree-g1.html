G1

Unitree · Humanoid · 2024

The Unitree G1 (May 2024) is a compact, affordable humanoid — the most-published-on commercial humanoid in academic research. It stands 1.32m/35kg with 23-43 DoF (configurable), walks at 2 m/s, and holds a 1.4m long-jump record, with backflips, side-flips and kip-ups. Base models have 90 N·m knee torque; EDU adds 120 N·m, Jetson Orin NX (100 TOPS), 3kg arm payload, dual encoders, LIVOX MID-360 3D
