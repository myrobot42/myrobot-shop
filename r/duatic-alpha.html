Alpha

Duatic · Humanoid · 2025

Duatic Alpha is a vision-enabled wheeled 'half-humanoid' — a dexterous dual-arm upper body (two DynaArms) on a strong mobile base, built for industrial logistics and manufacturing. It lifts ~30kg (each arm ~6kg continuous / 12kg burst) and its base supports payloads up to 200kg, handling floor-to-shelf pick-and-place, transport and assembly. Hot-swappable batteries enable near-continuous operation
