Altius 700M

Anduril · Drones · 2024

Anduril's ALTIUS-700M is a tube-launched, fixed-wing loitering munition with ~75 minutes endurance, up to 160 km range and a 15 kg anti-armor warhead. Launchable from air, ground or maritime platforms, it supports autonomous coordinated-strike and collaborative teaming so one operator can control multiple vehicles.
