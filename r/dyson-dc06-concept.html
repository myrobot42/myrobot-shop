Dyson DC06 Robot Concept

Dyson · Consumer · 2001

Dyson's first-ever robot vacuum - the DC06, announced in 2001 (a year before the Roomba). It packed 80 onboard sensors for mapping and navigation but weighed a daunting ~20lb (3x a typical robovac), vacuumed poorly, and carried a projected $3,000-6,000 price, so Dyson never released it. It began a 16-year robotics program.
