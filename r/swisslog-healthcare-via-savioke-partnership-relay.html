Relay

Swisslog Healthcare · Medical · 2014

The Relay robot (originally from Savioke, deployed in healthcare via Swisslog) is a compact autonomous delivery robot that carries medications, specimens and small items through hospitals, navigating autonomously and operating elevators for secure point-to-point delivery.
