WABOT-2

Waseda University · Humanoid · 1984

WABOT-2, introduced in 1984 by Waseda University's Kato laboratory, was the world's first musician humanoid robot — a 50-degrees-of-freedom machine that could read a printed musical score with its CCD-camera head and play a Yamaha FX-1 electronic keyboard using both hands and feet, even adjusting its performance to accompany a human singer.
