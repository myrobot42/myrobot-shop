LUF 60

LUF GmbH · Disaster Response · 2018

LUF GmbH's LUF 60 is an Austrian tracked firefighting robot built around a powerful turbine fan that throws an atomised water/foam mist tens of metres. Remotely operated, it excels in tunnels, underground car parks and confined industrial spaces where heat, smoke and collapse risk keep crews out, also serving as a high-capacity smoke extractor.
