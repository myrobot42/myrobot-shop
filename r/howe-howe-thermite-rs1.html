Thermite RS1

Howe & Howe · Disaster Response · 2019

The Howe & Howe Thermite RS1 is a compact remote firefighting robot on tank treads with a diesel powerplant, designed to enter structures and industrial fires too dangerous for crews and deliver high-volume water/foam streams.
