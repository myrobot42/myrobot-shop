Canadarm3

Canadian Space Agency / MDA · Aerospace · 2030

Canadarm3 is Canada's AI-enabled robotic system for NASA's Lunar Gateway - a smart, largely autonomous arm (built by MDA) that will maintain, repair and inspect the Gateway station in lunar orbit without constant human control.
