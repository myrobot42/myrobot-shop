Calvin-40

Wandercraft · Humanoid · 2025

Calvin-40, from French robotics company Wandercraft, is a self-balancing bipedal humanoid prototype notable for its origin story — reportedly designed and built in about 40 days (hence the name), leveraging the company's decade-plus of expertise in dynamic-walking exoskeletons. Wandercraft is best known for Atalante, a self-balancing medical exoskeleton that lets paralysed patients stand and walk
