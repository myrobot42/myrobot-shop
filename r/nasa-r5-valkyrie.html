Valkyrie (R5)

NASA · Humanoid · 2013

Full-size bipedal humanoid built by NASA's Johnson Space Center in just 15 months for the 2013 DARPA Robotics Challenge. Designed for potential deployment in space exploration scenarios including pre-deployment site preparation on Mars. Several units placed at research institutions (MIT, IHMC, Northeastern) for continued development.
