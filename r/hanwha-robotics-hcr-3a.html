HCR-3A

Hanwha Robotics · Industrial · 2019

The Hanwha HCR-3A is the compact entry to Hanwha Robotics' HCR cobot line — a 3 kg-payload, 630 mm-reach 6-axis arm weighing 13 kg on a Ø128 mm base, built for confined spaces where humans struggle to work. Its standout mechanics are wrist speed (J4/J5 at 360°/s) and an infinite-rotation J6, rare in this class, with ±0.05 mm repeatability and 1 m/s TCP speed. The quietest of the family at ≤60 dBA
