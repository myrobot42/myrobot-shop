Spot

Boston Dynamics · Quadruped · 2019

Spot is Boston Dynamics' commercial quadruped robot — a 12-DOF four-legged platform with 360° perception from five stereo camera pairs, built for autonomous industrial inspection, predictive maintenance and hazardous-area work. It carries 14kg of payload, walks at 1.6 m/s, climbs stairs and 300mm obstacles, handles ±30° slopes, and runs ~90 minutes per charge (60-min recharge, hot-swap batteries).
