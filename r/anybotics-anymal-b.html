ANYmal B

ANYbotics · Quadruped · 2016

The research-generation ANYmal, developed at ETH Zurich's Robotic Systems Lab and commercialised by ANYbotics. Driven by torque-controllable ANYdrive series-elastic actuators, it walks, trots, climbs stairs and recovers from falls, and served as the foundation for the later industrial ANYmal C/D/X inspection systems.
