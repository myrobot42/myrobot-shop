OP3

ROBOTIS · Humanoid · 2025

An affordable, fully open-source miniature humanoid research/education platform from ROBOTIS — the successor to the widely-used DARwIn-OP / OP2. It has 20 DYNAMIXEL XM430 actuator modules (6-DoF legs x2, 3-DoF arms x2, 2-DoF neck) with current-based control on a 4.5 Mbps bus, an OpenCR (ARM Cortex-M7) real-time controller, and an Intel NUC Core i3 with 8 GB DDR4 and an M.2 SSD for onboard compute.
