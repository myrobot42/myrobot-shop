Zu 18

JAKA · Industrial · 2021

The JAKA Zu 18 is a 6-axis collaborative robot (18 kg payload, 1073 mm reach) from Shanghai-based JAKA, featuring wireless drag-and-drop/graphical programming, collision detection and fenceless operation for pick-and-place, assembly, machine tending and palletizing.
