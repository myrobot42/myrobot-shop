A1

AGIBOT · Humanoid · 2023

AGIBOT's RAISE A1 (Aug 2023) was the company's first humanoid - a 175 cm, 53 kg, 49-DOF industrial biped that walks at ~7 km/h and handles loads up to 80 kg. Equipped with RGB-D and LiDAR sensors and modular components, it targets automotive, 3C-manufacturing, logistics and laboratory tasks like bolt-tightening and vehicle inspection.
