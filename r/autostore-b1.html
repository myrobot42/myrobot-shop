B1 (Black Line)

AutoStore · AGV/AMR · 2019

Lighter, faster Black Line grid robot with a cavity design roughly half the footprint of the R5. Uses individual direct-drive wheel motors and a swappable BattPack lithium-ion battery, giving about 75% higher acceleration and ~20% higher bin throughput than the R5, and can carry taller bins.
