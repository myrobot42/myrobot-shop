Burro Verde

Burro · Agricultural · 2019

Purpose-built Burro variant for autonomous transport to and from greenhouses. Seamlessly transitions between narrow indoor pathways and outdoor roadways using LIDAR. Can tow up to 907 kg (2,000 lb) and carry up to 227 kg (500 lb) on flat concrete. Designed as a collaborative robot for greenhouse farmworkers, replacing manual transport using tractors, gators, or golf carts.
