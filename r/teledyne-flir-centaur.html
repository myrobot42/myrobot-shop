Centaur

Teledyne FLIR · Military · 2019

The Teledyne FLIR Centaur is a ~73 kg medium UGV chosen by the US Army as its MTRS Increment II solution, with over 1,800 ordered across the Army, Navy, Air Force and Marines. Its EO/IR camera suite and manipulator arm (reaching over 6 ft) let EOD teams defeat IEDs, UXO and landmines at standoff, and modular payloads add CBRN detection.
