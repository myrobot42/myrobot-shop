BellaBot

Pudu Robotics · Delivery · 2020

Pudu's BellaBot is the iconic cat-faced restaurant delivery robot - a 4-tray food runner (40 kg load, 10 kg per layer) with expressive multi-modal interaction. It uses dual Laser + Visual SLAM with three RGBD depth cameras and lidar, stopping within 0.5 s on obstacles, and runs 12-24 hours on a swappable battery. Up to 20 units coordinate via PUDU SCHEDULER for busy dining rooms.
