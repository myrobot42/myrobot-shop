RT-20 (MTGR)

RoboTeam · Military · 2019

RoboTeam's MTGR (Micro Tactical Ground Robot, RT-20) is a rugged ~9 kg backpackable tracked robot with high manoeuvrability indoors and out. Widely used by the IDF in urban and tunnel environments, its intuitive ROCU control and modular payloads make it a go-to for military, law-enforcement and SWAT reconnaissance.
