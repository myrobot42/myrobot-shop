Oli

LimX Dynamics · Humanoid · 2026

The LimX Oli is a full-size general-purpose humanoid robot from LimX Dynamics (Shenzhen), offered in four configurations — Oli Lite, EDU, Super and a taller 175cm model — sharing a common platform (following a GSMArena-style single-SKU-with-variants model). The 165cm models stand 165cm at ≤55kg with 31-33 DOF (6 per leg, 7 per arm, 3 waist, 2 neck); the 175cm flagship reaches 43 DOF, ≤60kg, 250 N·
