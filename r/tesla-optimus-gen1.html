Optimus Gen 1

Tesla · Humanoid · 2023

Optimus Gen 1 (Sept 2022, 'Bumblebee'/first working prototype) was Tesla's initial functional humanoid — 1.73m, 73kg — shown walking and performing simple tasks at Tesla AI Day. It established the platform Tesla would rapidly lighten and refine into Gen 2, using Tesla-designed actuators and vision AI derived from Full Self-Driving.
