FX-2

Rainbow Robotics · Service · 2017

FX-2, from KAIST's HUBO Lab and Rainbow Robotics, is a large-scale rideable bipedal humanoid — a 'human-riding' robot that walks with a person seated aboard, the successor to the FX-1 model developed in 2013.
