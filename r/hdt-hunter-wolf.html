Hunter WOLF

HDT Global · Military · 2020

Rugged 6x6 hybrid-electric squad support UGV (Wheeled Offload Logistics Follower) built to carry heavy loads, evacuate casualties and mount a remote weapon station for overwatch. Fielded by US Army units including the 101st Airborne Division, where it has been operated with a remotely-controlled .50-calibre machine gun during combat training. Designed for last-tactical-mile resupply and dismounted
