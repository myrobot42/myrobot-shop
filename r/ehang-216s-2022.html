EH216-S

EHang · Drones · 2022

The EHang 216S is a two-seat autonomous passenger eVTOL with 16 rotors on eight arms. Pilotless and command-and-control flown, it carries ~220 kg (two passengers) at up to ~130 km/h over ~35 km for urban air mobility and sightseeing. EHang received a CAAC type certificate for the EH216-S, a milestone toward commercial autonomous passenger flight.
