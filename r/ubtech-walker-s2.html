Walker S2

UBTECH · Humanoid · 2025

The UBTech Walker S2 is a full-size industrial humanoid (about 1.76 m, 70 kg, 52 degrees of freedom) and the headline successor to UBTech's factory-deployed Walker S line, which works on NIO and BYD assembly lines.
