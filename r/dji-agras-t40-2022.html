Agras T40

DJI · Drones · 2022

The DJI Agras T40 (2022) is a heavy agricultural spray drone with a coaxial twin-rotor design, a 40 L spray tank (50 kg spreading capacity) and a dual-atomising spray system. Spherical radar and binocular vision enable terrain-following and obstacle avoidance for automated spraying and spreading over large fields.
