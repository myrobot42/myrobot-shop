VR-H3

VinRobotics · Humanoid · 2026

The VR-H3 is the third-generation industrial humanoid from VinRobotics, the robotics arm of Vietnamese conglomerate Vingroup. Standing 1.78 m and weighing 75 kg, it uses 31 smart actuators with rotary joints to replicate human movement, and carries two hot-swappable batteries that can each be swapped individually without powering the robot down.
