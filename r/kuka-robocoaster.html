RoboCoaster

KUKA · Entertainment · 2015

A passenger-carrying amusement ride built from a KUKA industrial robot arm — the concept was invented by Gino De-Gol (RoboCoaster Ltd, England) and realised with KUKA in 2001 from the adapted KR500 robot, debuting in 2002 with ten units sold to Legoland Denmark.
