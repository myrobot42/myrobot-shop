Alex

Boardwalk Robotics · Humanoid · 2024

A commercial humanoid upper-torso robot from Boardwalk Robotics (spun out of Florida's IHMC, of DARPA Robotics Challenge fame), announced August 2024. Alex deliberately omits legs — mounted on a stable base, it can't fall or face-plant, letting it work safely close to people and move its arms far faster than a balancing biped.
