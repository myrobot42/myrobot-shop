Prowler 730

Pentair · Consumer · 2021

The above-ground member of Pentair's Prowler robotic pool-cleaner family. A corded, plug-and-play cleaner that scrubs and vacuums the floor and cove of above-ground pools with a top-access filter basket, running independently of the pool's filtration system. Self-programming navigation and an included caddy make it a drop-in-and-go cleaner.
