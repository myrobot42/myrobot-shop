Monarch Platform

Johnson & Johnson MedTech · Medical · 2018

The Monarch Platform (J&J MedTech, originally Auris Health) is a robotic flexible-endoscopy system using a controller-driven scope for peripheral lung biopsy (bronchoscopy) and kidney-stone removal (urology), with navigation guidance.
