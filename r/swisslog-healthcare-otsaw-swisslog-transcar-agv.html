TransCar AGV

Swisslog Healthcare · AGV/AMR · 2018

Hospital automated guided vehicle from the OTSAW–Swisslog Healthcare joint venture that travels predetermined paths to deliver supplies, medication, linens, meals and waste 24/7, freeing staff for patient care. A tricycle drive system and lift deck with photocell sensors give precise container handling in tight spaces, and it integrates with elevators, auto-doors and hospital information systems v
