Honor · Humanoid · 2026

Yuanqi Zai (元气仔, 'Spirit Kid') is Honor's compact consumer-facing humanoid — the product it actually intends to commercialise, versus its flagship Lightning racer. At 136.9 cm with a friendly silver design, it is built for human-robot interaction and companionship, with Honor planning to deploy it across ~5,000 of its own retail stores as an in-store greeter and shopping guide.
