Beomni 1.0

Beyond Imagination · Humanoid · 2021

A wheeled, teleoperated general-purpose humanoid from Beyond Imagination — the startup co-founded in 2018 by futurist Ray Kurzweil and scientist Harry Kloor. Beomni pairs a human-like upper body (articulated head, shoulders, waist, two arms and dexterous hands with opposable thumbs) with a four-wheel base that traverses mud, sand and snow. Each arm lifts up to ~15.9 kg yet can pinch a bit of salt
