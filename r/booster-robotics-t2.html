T2

Booster Robotics · Humanoid · 2026

The Booster T2 is a humanoid robot from Booster Robotics, a Chinese developer known for accessible, sport-capable bipeds. The T2 competed in the autonomous category of the 2026 Beijing E-Town Humanoid Half-Marathon. It follows the company's earlier T1 platform, which is popular with research and education teams and in robot-football competitions.
