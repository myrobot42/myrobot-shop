Elf

BXI Robotics · Humanoid · 2025

An open-source, human-sized bipedal humanoid from Shanghai's BXI Robotics, aimed at sim-to-real deployment and locomotion-algorithm research. The Elf stands 1.45 m and weighs just 32 kg, making it easy to carry, with 25 degrees of freedom (6 per leg, 5 per arm, 3 in the waist).
