Mirumi

Yukai Engineering · Social · 2025

A palm-sized, furry companion robot from Tokyo's Yukai Engineering (the studio behind Qoobo and Nekojita FuFu), shown at CES 2025. Mirumi clips onto a bag handle or strap and does exactly one thing charmingly: it turns its single rotating head to 'peek' at nearby people.
