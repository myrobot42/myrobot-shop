Outrider · Autonomous Vehicles · 2022

Outrider's system automates the distribution yard - an electric autonomous yard truck that moves, spots and hitches trailers, connects brake lines and handles repetitive yard tasks without a driver. Operating at low speed within geofenced private yards, it combines an electric yard tractor with Outrider's L4 autonomy and lidar-based perception.
