Ginger

Dataa Robotics · Humanoid · 2019

A cloud-connected humanoid service robot from Dataa Robotics (formerly CloudMinds), first shown as the XR-1 at MWC 2019 and a 2020 Red Star Design Award winner. Its intelligence runs largely in the HARIX cloud brain over 5G, giving image, object, posture, face and emotion recognition, multilingual multi-round dialogue and cloud-scheduled multi-robot collaboration.
