PackBot 525

Teledyne FLIR · Military · 2022

Latest generation of the battle-proven, man-transportable PackBot tracked UGV. Deploys in under two minutes, climbs stairs and rubble, and performs EOD, ISR, CBRN detection and HazMat handling. The 525 adds HD cameras, improved illumination, a laser rangefinder and optional in-situ charging. Over 3,000 PackBots have been fielded across 57 countries; in 2022 Teledyne FLIR delivered 127 PackBot 525s
