Bot Handy

Samsung · Humanoid · 2021

A household-assistant robot concept from Samsung Research, first shown at CES 2021 and upgraded at CES 2022. Bot Handy pairs a wheeled mobile base with a tall, slim body and a single highly-articulated arm that pivots at shoulder, elbow and wrist, ending in a clamp-like hand.
