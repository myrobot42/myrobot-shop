CIMON

Airbus / DLR · Aerospace · 2018

CIMON (Crew Interactive Mobile Companion) is a free-floating, AI-powered spherical assistant aboard the ISS, built by Airbus for DLR with IBM Watson voice AI - it talks with astronauts, displays procedures and floats via internal fans.
