D1

Direct Drive Technology · Quadruped · 2025

The D1 from Hong Kong's Direct Drive Technology is billed as the world's first fully modular embodied-intelligence robot. Two wheel-legged biped units (24.3 kg each) dock via a central magnetic coupler to form a 48.6 kg wheel-legged quadruped 'robot dog', then separate again autonomously - a capability the company calls 'All-Domain Splicing'. Each unit runs an NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX (8 GB) on Ubunt
