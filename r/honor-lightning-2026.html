Lightning (闪电)

Honor · Humanoid · 2026

Lightning (闪电) is a high-speed bipedal humanoid built by Chinese smartphone maker Honor, which stunned the robotics world by winning the 2026 Beijing E-Town Humanoid Half-Marathon. Running the 21.1 km course autonomously in 50 minutes 26 seconds, it became the first humanoid to beat the human half-marathon world record (57:20); a remote-controlled Lightning finished even faster at 48:19.
