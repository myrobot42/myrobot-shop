TracePen

Wandelbots · Research · 2017

The Wandelbots TracePen is a handheld teaching device (a stylus-like tool) that lets operators program industrial robots by demonstration - tracing a path in physical space rather than coding - to dramatically speed up robot setup. An accessory/teaching tool rather than a robot itself.
