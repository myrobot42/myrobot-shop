ScanEagle

Boeing · Drones · 2004

A small, long-endurance ISR drone from Insitu (a Boeing subsidiary), derived from the commercial SeaScan fish-spotting UAV and in service since the mid-2000s. Its composite, high-aspect-ratio flying wing (3.1 m span, ~1.7 m long, ~26.5 kg) rides a small ~1.1 kW two-stroke, heavy-fuel-capable piston engine driving a pusher prop, giving 18-24 hours of endurance (a Block D set a 22h 8m record) at ~90
