LEMUR 2

BRINC · Drones · 2024

BRINC's LEMUR 2 is a purpose-built public-safety drone for indoor, GPS-denied tactical missions. It carries a 4K camera, FLIR thermal, IR/white spotlights, two-way audio and a glass-breaker, with a 10-sensor Autonomy Engine and onboard LiDAR generating real-time 3D floor plans and a light/GPS-free 360-degree hover. AES-256-encrypted, mesh-networked and 4G-connected for live streaming via BRINC Liv
