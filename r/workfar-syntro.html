Syntro

WorkFar Technologies · Humanoid · 2024

Syntro by WorkFar Technologies (Dover, Delaware) is a teleoperated 'sentient' humanoid leased as a human-robot team on a monthly basis (robot-as-a-service) to logistics and manufacturing businesses. A remote operator wears VR goggles with AR overlays; eye tracking, VR and haptic/force feedback transmit the robot's environment back in real time, supplemented by onboard AI.
