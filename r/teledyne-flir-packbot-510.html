PackBot 510

Teledyne FLIR Defense · Military · 2010

The Teledyne FLIR PackBot 510 is the iconic man-transportable tracked EOD robot - deployable by one person in under two minutes, with flippers for stairs and rough terrain and a manipulator that lifts 20 kg. More than 5,000 PackBots have been delivered, with nearly 2,000 of the 510 used in Iraq and Afghanistan for bomb disposal, recon and CBRN work.
