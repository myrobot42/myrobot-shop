Aerial Firefighter Drone

Erickson Aerial · Disaster Response · 2020

An aerial fire-inspection drone associated with Erickson, the aerial-firefighting company best known for its Air-Crane helicopters. Carrying thermal and visual cameras, it provides aerial reconnaissance and fire mapping to give incident commanders real-time situational awareness over wildfire and structure-fire scenes.
