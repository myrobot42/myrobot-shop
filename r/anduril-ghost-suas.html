Ghost (Anduril sUAS)

Anduril Industries · Drones · 2021

Anduril's first drone: a soldier-portable, single-rotor helicopter-style VTOL small UAS built for quiet, modular ISR, targeting and force protection at the tactical edge. Running at low rotor speed for a near-silent acoustic signature, the ~17 kg Ghost 4 carries modular payloads — a Trillium EO/IR gimbal plus optional SIGINT, EW and radar — swappable in seconds, and is driven by Anduril's Lattice
