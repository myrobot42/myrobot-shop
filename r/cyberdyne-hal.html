HAL (Hybrid Assistive Limb)

Cyberdyne · Exoskeletons · 2012

Cyberdyne's HAL (Hybrid Assistive Limb) reads faint bioelectric signals on the skin to detect intended motion and powers the hip and knee joints accordingly. Versions exist for medical lower-limb therapy, single-joint, and lumbar (care/industrial) support.
