Multifunctional tracked support UGV codified by Ukraine's Ministry of Defence in June 2025, able to carry up to 300 kg across rough terrain, slopes, obstacles and trenches. Its low tracked profile suits routes where wheeled supply vehicles are too visible, and it reconfigures for medical evacuation, logistics resupply, mine-laying and equipment transport for front-line units.
