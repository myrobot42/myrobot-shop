Rhyno Protect

Shark Robotics · Disaster Response · 2020

Shark Robotics' Rhyno Protect is a tracked civil-protection robot designed for tactical and law-enforcement use. It can carry a ballistic shield to provide mobile cover, extract wounded people from danger zones, and perform reconnaissance in active-threat or hazardous situations, keeping responders out of the line of fire.
