ReStore

ReWalk Robotics · Exoskeletons · 2019

ReStore is a soft, lightweight exo-suit from ReWalk (Lifeward) for stroke rehabilitation, using cable-driven assistance to support ankle dorsiflexion and plantarflexion during gait therapy.
