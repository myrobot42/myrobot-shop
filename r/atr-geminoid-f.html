Geminoid F

Hiroshi Ishiguro Lab · Humanoid · 2010

Geminoid F ('F' for female), unveiled in 2010 by roboticist Hiroshi Ishiguro (ATR and Osaka University) with animatronics firm Kokoro, is one of the world's most famous ultra-realistic androids — a copy of a real woman in her twenties, with silicone skin and real human hair over a fiber-reinforced-plastic skull.
