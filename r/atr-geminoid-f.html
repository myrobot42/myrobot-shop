Geminoid F

Hiroshi Ishiguro Lab · Humanoid · 2010

A teleoperated android copy of a real woman, built by Kokoro and Hiroshi Ishiguro (ATR/Osaka University, 2010). Pneumatically actuated with silicone skin and real human hair, Geminoid F autonomously breathes and blinks and mirrors a remote operator's expressions — a landmark in uncanny-realistic HRI research.
