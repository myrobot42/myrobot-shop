TurtleBot 4

Clearpath Robotics · Research · 2022

TurtleBot 4 is the world's leading low-cost open-source ROS 2 mobile robot, built on iRobot's Create 3 base with a Raspberry Pi 4, 2D LiDAR and OAK-D camera. The de-facto entry platform for learning ROS 2; available in Lite and Standard.
