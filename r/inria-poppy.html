Poppy

INRIA / Poppy Project · Humanoid · 2014

Poppy, created by INRIA's Flowers team in Bordeaux (born 2012 out of Matthieu Lapeyre's PhD under Pierre-Yves Oudeyer, funded by an ERC grant), is the first complete open-source, 3D-printed humanoid robot — a platform designed so scientists, students, engineers and artists can fast and flexibly explore robot morphologies. About 840 mm tall and only ~3.5 kg, it has 25 DOF driven by Robotis Dynamixe
