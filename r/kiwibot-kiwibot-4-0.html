Kiwibot 4.0

Kiwibot · Delivery · 2021

Kiwibot 4.0 is a compact four-wheeled campus food-delivery robot from the Berkeley-based startup, navigating with LiDAR and stereo vision (full autonomy in mapped zones, remote assist elsewhere). IP54 weather resistance, partnerships with Segway, Chick-fil-A and Shopify, and a fleet that has surpassed 150,000 deliveries across dozens of US universities.
