FD20

FarmDroid · Agricultural · 2021

The FarmDroid FD20 is a fully solar-powered autonomous robot that both seeds and mechanically weeds row crops. It records each seed's RTK-GPS position (8 mm) at sowing, so it can weed between and within rows before plants even emerge. ~1,250 kg (low soil compaction), 18-24 h/day CO2-neutral operation, ~6 ha/day, 50+ crop types; the +Spray option cuts chemicals up to 94%.
