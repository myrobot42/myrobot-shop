DR01

DEEP Robotics · Humanoid · 2024

The DR01 is DEEP Robotics' first humanoid, positioned as an 'embodied intelligence explorer' from the Hangzhou company best known for its industrial quadrupeds (Lite3, X20, X30). Standing about 1.7 m and weighing ~80 kg, the DR01 is a locomotion-focused biped with 12 degrees of freedom driven by DEEP's self-developed lightweight J60 and high-power J100 joints.
