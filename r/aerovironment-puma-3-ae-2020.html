Puma 3 AE

AeroVironment · Drones · 2020

AeroVironment's RQ-20 Puma 3 AE is a hand-launched, all-environment fixed-wing ISR drone with a stabilised EO/IR gimbal, ~2.5 hours endurance (with long-endurance battery) and ~20 km range. Waterproof for land or maritime operations, it provides persistent tactical reconnaissance for ground forces.
