Delivery Robot Gen 5

Starship Technologies · Delivery · 2018

Starship's six-wheeled autonomous sidewalk delivery robot - the most-deployed in the world with 10M+ deliveries. It travels at pedestrian pace (~6 km/h) within ~2-3 km of a store, carrying up to 10 kg (about three shopping bags) in an app-locked insulated compartment. A bogie/six-wheel system climbs curbs; perception uses 12 cameras plus ultrasonic, radar and GPS. Operates at L4 with remote human
