HRP-2 (Promet)

AIST · Humanoid · 2002

HRP-2 (Promet), unveiled in 2002, is one of the most influential research humanoids ever built — the flagship of Japan's national Humanoid Robotics Project (AIST with Kawada Industries and Kawasaki Heavy Industries, under NEDO/METI).
