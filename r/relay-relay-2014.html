Relay

Relay Robotics · Delivery · 2014

The Relay robot (from Relay Robotics, formerly Savioke - which pioneered hotel delivery as 'Botlr' at Aloft in 2014) is a compact enclosed-bin delivery robot. With a ~10-gallon secure compartment, it delivers room-service items in hotels and lab specimens, medications and supplies in hospitals, autonomously calling and riding elevators and returning to its dock to recharge between trips.
