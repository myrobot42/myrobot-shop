Inspire 2

DJI · Drones · 2016

The DJI Inspire 2 (2016) is a professional cinematography platform with interchangeable Zenmuse X5S/X7 cameras, recording up to 5.2K CinemaDNG RAW and Apple ProRes. Its transforming airframe raises the landing gear for unobstructed 360-degree shots, and dual-battery power gives ~27 minutes of flight with dual-operator control.
