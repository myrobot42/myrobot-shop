Digit v1

Agility Robotics · Humanoid · 2019

Digit v1 (2019) was Agility Robotics' first Digit — adding a torso, arms and sensing to the Cassie leg platform to create a bipedal robot for logistics research and early pilots. It introduced the arms-plus-legs configuration that all later Digits build on.
