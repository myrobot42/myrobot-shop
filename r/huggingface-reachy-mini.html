Reachy Mini

Hugging Face · Research · 2025

Reachy Mini (Hugging Face, from Pollen Robotics) is a low-cost open-source expressive desktop robot for embodied-AI development - a moving/animated head with camera, mics and speaker, programmable in Python and integrated with the Hugging Face Hub. A May 2026 agentic toolkit lets users build working apps in under an hour with no manual coding. Lite (~99) and wireless (~49) versions.
