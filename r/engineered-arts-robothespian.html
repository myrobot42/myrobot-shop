RoboThespian

Engineered Arts · Service · 2005

Engineered Arts' RoboThespian is a life-size interactive humanoid robot built for visitor attractions, science centres and education. Highly expressive through gestures, speech and projected facial animation, and remotely operable for telepresence, it has been one of the most widely deployed entertainment/education humanoids worldwide.
