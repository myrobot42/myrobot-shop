RoboThespian

Engineered Arts · Humanoid · 2005

The pioneering 'robot actor' from England's Engineered Arts (founded 2004), first built in 2005 and now installed at 250+ venues worldwide including NASA Kennedy Space Center and museums. RoboThespian is a life-size (1.75 m, 33 kg) interactive humanoid designed for public display: an aluminium chassis and PET body shell house an actuated upper body, arms, hands, head and jaw driven by Festo pneuma
