SORA-Q (LEV-2)

JAXA · Aerospace · 2024

SORA-Q (LEV-2) is JAXA's baseball-sized (~250 g) transformable lunar rover deployed by the SLIM "Moon Sniper" lander (Jan 2024). It splits open into a two-wheeled crawler to image the lander and terrain - co-developed with toy maker Tomy, Sony and Doshisha University.
