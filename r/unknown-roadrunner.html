Roadrunner

RAI Institute · Humanoid · 2026

The RAI Institute Roadrunner (launched March 2026) is a 15 kg bipedal-wheeled robot prototype from Marc Raibert's Robotics and AI Institute. Its symmetrical knee-jointed legs end in wheels, giving multi-modal locomotion - side-by-side and in-line wheel modes plus stepping - so it can roll on flat ground and climb stairs. A single learned control policy handles all modes, deployed zero-shot via sim
