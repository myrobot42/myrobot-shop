DiDi RoboRide

DiDi · Autonomous Vehicles · 2023

DiDi Autonomous Driving's robotaxi, co-developed with GAC Aion on the Aion V SUV. The first mass-produced L4 model integrates 33 sensors in a 360° blind-spot-free fusion architecture, including four Hesai AT128 lidars (1.53M points/sec, 200 m range) and 4D imaging radar. Deployed via the DiDi app in Guangzhou with Beijing road-test approval.
