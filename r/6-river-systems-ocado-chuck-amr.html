Chuck

6 River Systems (Ocado Group) · AGV/AMR · 2017

Collaborative mobile picking robot — resembling a pick cart — that leads warehouse associates through picking, putaway, sorting and returns, navigating autonomously and guiding workers via an 11-inch touchscreen for roughly 2–3× the productivity of manual cart picking. Built by ex-Kiva founders; now the core of the Ocado Mobile Robot System after Ocado's 2023 acquisition. Twice a Red Dot Award win
