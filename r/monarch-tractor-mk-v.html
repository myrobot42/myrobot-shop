MK-V

Monarch Tractor · Agricultural · 2023

The Monarch MK-V is a 70 hp fully-electric driver-optional smart tractor: up to ~14 h battery (5-6 h recharge), IP65, 4WD, swappable battery packs and onboard power outlets, with camera-based autonomy and per-acre data collection. Suited to vineyards, orchards, dairy and solar-farm vegetation management; cuts ~2 t CO2/year on a 50-acre farm.
