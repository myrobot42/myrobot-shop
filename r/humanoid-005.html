Apollo

Apptronik · Humanoid · 2024

Apollo is Apptronik's general-purpose commercial humanoid — a 1.73m, ~73kg robot with a 25kg payload, ~28 DoF, and hot-swappable ~4-hour battery packs, engineered for continuous industrial operation alongside people. It uses Apptronik's proprietary linear electric actuators (developed over 13+ generations) with force control for safe collaboration, a chest-mounted main computer, and a modular arch
