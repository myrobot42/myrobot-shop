AELOS PRO

Leju Robotics · Humanoid · 2019

The Leju AELOS Pro is a 19-degree-of-freedom educational humanoid robot with high-precision servos for lifelike walking, dancing and martial-arts moves. A built-in HD camera enables facial recognition and object tracking, 14+ sensor ports allow custom builds, and it programs via Blockly drag-and-drop or Python - bridging beginner to advanced STEM and competition robotics.
