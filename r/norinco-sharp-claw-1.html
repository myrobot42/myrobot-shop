Sharp Claw 1

NORINCO · Military · 2014

Small lightweight (~120 kg) tracked scout-and-strike UGV designed for anti-terror, patrol and urban-battle missions, able to climb stairs and clear buildings and tunnels. Armed with a 7.62 mm machine gun (effective to ~500 m) and a sensor package for all-round surveillance, controlled by radio to ~800 m.
