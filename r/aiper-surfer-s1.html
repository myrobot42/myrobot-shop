Surfer S1

Aiper · Consumer · 2024

A cordless, solar-powered robotic pool skimmer that glides across the water surface collecting floating leaves, pollen, insects and other debris into a 5 L top-load basket. Twin brushless paddle-wheel motors drive it while ultrasonic detectors recognise and avoid pool walls for edge-to-edge coverage, and Aiper's Sunseeker algorithm steers it toward sunlight to recharge.
