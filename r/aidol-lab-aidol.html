AIDOL

AIDOL Lab · Humanoid · 2025

AIDOL (by Artificial Intelligence Dynamic Organism Lab) is billed as Russia's first humanoid robot — notable for its debut where it stumbled and fell on stage, sparking wide coverage. Its face uses 19 servo motors and it has a 7-microphone array, enabling recognition of at least 12 basic emotions. It uses ~77% domestic components with a stated goal of raising local content to 93%.
