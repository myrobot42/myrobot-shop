MK30

Amazon Prime Air · Drones · 2024

Amazon Prime Air's MK30 (2024) is a hybrid VTOL fixed-wing delivery drone with six propulsors and staggered tandem wings. Smaller, quieter (~25% less perceived noise) and more durable than the MK27-2, it carries up to ~2.3 kg, cruises at ~33 m/s within ~12 km, flies in light rain, and drops packages from ~4 m without landing.
