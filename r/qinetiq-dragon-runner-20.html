Dragon Runner 20

QinetiQ · Disaster Response · 2012

Compact, rugged man-portable tracked robot carried in a backpack and deployable in seconds for reconnaissance, inspection and light EOD in confined spaces. The DR20 can be fitted with a manipulator arm; its smaller sibling, the throwable DR10, provides immediate situational awareness. Widely used by NATO EOD and dismounted units, including extensive UK service.
