Flex360 Robotic System

Medrobotics · Medical · 2014

The Medrobotics Flex Robotic System uses a steerable, articulating flexible scope to navigate non-linear anatomical pathways the rigid instruments of other systems cannot reach, enabling minimally invasive transoral (ENT) and colorectal procedures with surgeon-guided flexible access.
