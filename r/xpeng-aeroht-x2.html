AeroHT X2

XPeng AeroHT · Drones · 2022

A two-seat manned electric vertical-take-off aircraft from XPeng AeroHT (the flying-car affiliate of Chinese automaker XPeng), publicly flown in Dubai in 2022. The Voyager X2 is a closed-cockpit octacopter — eight rotors in a distributed-electric layout for stability and fail-safe redundancy — built around a lightweight carbon-fibre structure. It weighs about 560 kg empty, cruises around 130 km/h
