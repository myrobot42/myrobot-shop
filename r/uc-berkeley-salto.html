Salto

UC Berkeley · Research · 2016

A 100 g single-legged jumping robot from UC Berkeley (2016), inspired by the galago (bushbaby). Salto can do rapid, repeated wall-to-wall vertical leaps with the highest robotic 'vertical jumping agility' of its time — a benchmark in dynamic agile-locomotion research.
