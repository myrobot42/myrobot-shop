Thermite RS1

Howe & Howe Technologies · Disaster Response · 2014

Howe & Howe's Thermite RS1 (now Textron Systems) is a compact tracked firefighting robot for tight and confined spaces - doorways, entries and hallways. Remotely operated via a belly-pack controller with HD video, it flows up to 1,250 gpm through an Elkhart Brass Cobra monitor, letting crews fight fires from a safe standoff.
