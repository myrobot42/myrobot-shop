Kaleido (RHP7)

Kawasaki · Humanoid · 2022

Kawasaki's Kaleido (RHP — Robust Humanoid Platform) is the humanoid line from one of the world's oldest industrial-robot makers, in development since 2015 and first shown in 2017. This generation stands 1,780 mm at 85 kg with 32 DOF and unusually high industrial upper-body strength — handling loads up to 60 kg — while achieving fully untethered bipedal walking on an onboard battery.
