ExoMotus M4

Fourier Rehab · Exoskeletons · 2020

Fourier's ExoMotus M4 is a powered lower-limb gait-training exoskeleton paired with a wheeled self-balancing support frame, combining the benefits of treadmill-suspended rehab robots with real walking. Driven by in-house servo-motors and multi-dimensional force sensors, it supports early-stage sit-to-stand and ambulation retraining for individuals with lower-limb impairments.
