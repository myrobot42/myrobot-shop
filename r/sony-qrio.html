QRIO

Sony · Humanoid · 2003

Sony’s "Quest for cuRIOsity" — the first humanoid robot capable of running (officially certified by Guinness in 2005). The 58 cm QRIO walked, danced in troupes, threw balls, recognized faces and voices, and got up after falls; cancelled in 2006 alongside AIBO, never sold.
