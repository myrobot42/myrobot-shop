P1

LimX Dynamics · Humanoid · 2024

The LimX Dynamics P1 is an innovative point-foot biped robot — legs only, with no feet — that serves as LimX's platform for systematic development and modular testing of reinforcement-learning locomotion. Deliberately minimal, its point-foot design makes balance and control maximally challenging, which is exactly why it's valuable for RL research: mastering it proves the underlying algorithms. P1
