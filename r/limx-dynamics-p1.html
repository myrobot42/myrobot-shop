P1

LimX Dynamics · Research · 2023

P1 is LimX Dynamics' point-foot bipedal robot, known for reinforcement-learning demonstrations of robust untethered walking over rough outdoor terrain. It was the base from which LimX developed the motion-control algorithms behind its CL-1 humanoid, and a predecessor to the TRON modular biped line.
