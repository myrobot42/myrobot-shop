Andros F6B

Remotec (Northrop Grumman) · Disaster Response · 2018

Remotec's (Northrop Grumman) Andros F6B is a large, heavy-duty EOD platform with dual mobility - quick-release pneumatic wheels for a narrow profile plus patented articulating tracks for rough terrain and stairs. Its 7-degree-of-freedom manipulator arm offers high dexterity for render-safe procedures, making it a long-serving bomb-squad workhorse worldwide.
