Vikram

ISRO · Aerospace · 2023

Chandrayaan-3 lunar lander, named after Vikram Sarabhai. Soft-landed near the lunar south pole at Statio Shiv Shakti on 23 August 2023, making India the fourth nation to achieve a soft Moon landing and the first to land in the south polar region. Deployed the Pragyan rover. Improved design from failed Chandrayaan-2 Vikram with stronger legs and four (not five) throttlable engines.
