Diana 7 (by Agile Robots)

Agile Robots / Franka Robotics · Research · 2022

The Diana 7, from Agile Robots, is a 7-axis collaborative robot arm with torque sensors in every joint, a 7 kg payload, ~923 mm reach and ~0.05 mm repeatability. Its full force/torque sensing enables compliant, contact-rich manipulation, making it a popular platform for research and precision assembly.
