EksoNR

Ekso Bionics · Exoskeletons · 2020

EksoNR is Ekso Bionics' FDA-cleared lower-limb rehabilitation exoskeleton used in 400+ neuro-rehab clinics to help stroke, spinal-cord-injury and acquired-brain-injury patients relearn to walk. Its SmartAssist software adapts powered assistance at the hips and knees to each patient's effort.
