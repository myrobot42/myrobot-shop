THeMIS Support

Milrem Robotics · Military · 2014

The THeMIS Support configuration adapts Milrem's tracked UGV for combat-support roles - mounting remote weapon stations, ISR sensors or mission payloads on the modular 1,200 kg platform to extend infantry capability while keeping soldiers at a safer standoff.
