P40 Robot

Geekplus · AGV/AMR · 2020

Compact goods-to-person transport robot that carries totes and shelves to picking stations and travels under racks to save aisle space — about 20% faster than comparable robots. Pairs with RoboShuttle and the Robot Arm Picking Station. A vision-only variant — billed as the world's first — uses an Intel RealSense camera with V-SLAM to navigate curved paths without floor markers.
