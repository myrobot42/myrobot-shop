ROBAN

Leju Robotics · Educational · 2020

The Leju ROBAN is a 22-DOF medium-size bipedal humanoid for advanced education and research, featuring Leju's self-stabilising gait algorithms and torque servos. Programmable in Python with onboard vision, it is used in universities and competitions such as RoboCup to study humanoid locomotion, manipulation and AI.
