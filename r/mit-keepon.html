Keepon

BeatBots · Social · 2003

A small yellow snowman-shaped social robot (Hideki Kozima) designed to study social development with children, including autism-therapy research. Its minimal 4-DoF design conveys attention and emotion expressively; the bouncing 'Dancing Keepon' became a viral sensation and a retail toy.
